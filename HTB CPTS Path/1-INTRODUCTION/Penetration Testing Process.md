## Hack the box learning philosophy
	The philosophy is learn by doing following a risk-based approach with a heavy emphasis on hand-on learning and legal and ethical use of our skills. We learn the "why" behind a vulnerability and how to discover, exploit,remediate ,detect and prevent th flaw to create well rounded professionals who can pass this all-encompassing knowledge and mindset on to their current/future clients or employers to assist them in securing their people,technologies missions from modern cyber threats.
	Note: ALWAYS WORK ETHICALLY AND WITHIN THE BOUND OF THE LAW ,DOCUMENT DOCUMENT when in doubt and overcommunicate to the client
	 After completing this path i should focus on a particular specialisation such as active directory,web or revserse engineering

## Asking for help
	Instead of saying i can't solve X or tell me the answer to Y try approaching things diffrently. Before asking a question list out what you already know and what things you have already tried. in doing this you may find the answer is right in front of you.  **Asking good questions is a difficult skill to master , but it's necessary for us in our studies and careers**  

### Penetration testing overview

	 A penetratino test (pentest) is an organised, targeted and authorized attack attempt to test IT infrastructure and its defenders to determine their susceptibility to IT security vulnerabilities.
	 It aims to discover and identify all vulnerabilities in the systems under investigation and improve the security for the tested systems

### Precautionary Measures during Penetration Tests
| **Precautionary Measure** |                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `☐`                       | Obtain written consent from the owner or authorized representative of the computer or network being tested                                                     |
| `☐`                       | Conduct the testing within the scope of the consent obtained only and respect any limitations specified                                                        |
| `☐`                       | Take measures to prevent causing damage to the systems or networks being tested                                                                                |
| `☐`                       | Do not access, use or disclose personal data or any other information obtained during the testing without permission                                           |
| `☐`                       | Do not intercept electronic communications without the consent of one of the parties to the communication                                                      |
| `☐`                       | Do not conduct testing on systems or networks that are covered by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) without proper authorization |
## Practicing Steps

Think about the skills you have gained and what interests you the most from them. From there, we can pick out a few more modules to increase our knowledge, machines to practice on, and Pro Labs or Endgames to really put ourselves to the test. The numbers below are a good starting example:

- 2x Modules
- 3x Retired Machines
- 5x Active Machines
- 1x Pro Lab / Endgame
